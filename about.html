Our Story

A Folkestone Electrician You Can Actually Trust

Gilly's Electrical was built on a simple idea: people deserve an electrician who shows up when they say they will, does the job properly, and doesn't leave you guessing about the cost.

Based right here in Folkestone, we've spent years serving homeowners, landlords, and businesses across East Kent. We know the local area, we know the community, and we take real pride in the work we do for our neighbours.

This isn't a national franchise or a call centre operation. It's a local tradesman who cares about his reputation because he's going to see you at the shops. Every job — big or small — gets the same attention to detail and the same commitment to doing things right.

From EICR certificates for landlords to full rewires, consumer unit upgrades, fault finding, and ventilation systems, we cover the full range of electrical work. And when we're done, we leave your home exactly as we found it.
